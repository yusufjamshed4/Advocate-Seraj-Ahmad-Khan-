Frequently Asked Questions

Is legal verification important before buying land?
Yes, thorough title verification and checking Khatauni records are crucial to ensure the property is free from litigation, encumbrances, or boundary disputes.
How do I book an appointment for consultation?
You can call directly, message on WhatsApp, or fill out the consultation form below to schedule a meeting at the Phoolpur Tehsil office.
What documents are required for Khatauni name correction?
You will need previous sale deeds, identity proofs, family tree certificates, and current Khatauni extracts. Advocate Seraj Ahmad Khan can guide you through the exact paperwork.
